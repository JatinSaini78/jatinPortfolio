02Personal project · Social planning app

Linkle

A native SwiftUI app for finding small plans nearby, joining them, chatting with the group in real time, and hosting your own.

  • RoleiOS Developer
  • StatusComplete, not on the App Store
  • PlatformiPhone · iOS 18+
  • I built the core journey end to end: Discover, plan detail, join and group chat, including waitlists and the case where two people go for the last spot at the same time.
  • Group chat runs live on Firebase Realtime Database, with optimistic sending, an offline outbox that retries, polls, and access that ends the moment someone leaves.
  • The app is SwiftUI + MVVM, organised by feature: @Observable view models, repositories, dependency injection through the SwiftUI environment, and typed NavigationStack routing with deep links.
  • MapKit and CoreLocation show plans nearby, but only an approximate area until you join.
  • EventKit adds joined plans to your calendar, and there's PhotosUI, Sign in with Apple and local reminders too.
  • Unit tests with Swift Testing cover the business rules, like age checks, waitlists, plan states and polls.

Heads-up: plans and profiles run on a mock backend for now. Sign-in and chat are real Firebase.

03Personal project · Hydration tracker In development

HydraPet

A habit app where you pick an animal companion, and it fills up with water as you log drinks through the day.

  • PlatformiPhone · iOS 18+
  • 13 drink types are weighted by how hydrating they are, and there's a little celebration when you hit your goal.
  • Streaks follow your local calendar day, and if you miss one, a Bounce Back mode helps you get going again.
  • Challenges, companions you unlock, a weekly insights score and reminder notifications keep it a habit.
  • It's SwiftUI with @Observable view models over SwiftData repositories, with haptic feedback throughout.
  • A language picker switches the whole app live, including Arabic with right-to-left layouts.
  • Unit tests with Swift Testing cover streaks, challenges and the switch to a new day.
